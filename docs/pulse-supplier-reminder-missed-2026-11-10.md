## Permanent code remediation
- Added date-window-scoped Israel-local event inventory with full pagination of all relevant EventServices and Suppliers; no need for a broad every-service-on-every-day query. Used by dailyScheduledNotifications and sendEventReminders.
- Existing event reminder scheduled fanout now paginates services and suppliers, uses one event-supplier reminder per recipient instead of once per EventService row, and uses the same business idempotency key as the daily and legacy sender.
- InApp notifications are only mirrors; their existence is not proof WhatsApp was enqueued. The same durable Outbox business key is offered without creating duplicates. Already accepted or uncertain key outcomes remain non-replayable.
- Removed only invisible Unicode directional presentation markers in canonical WhatsApp phone parsing, preserving all digits and rejecting extensions, invalid formats, URLs and second numbers.
- Corrected the legacy day-before event reminder cutoff to use local start-of-day rather than the event start time.
- Added a one-time owner-requested hold for 11 Nov 2026 event reminders during 10–11 Nov in all three event-reminder producers, automatically expiring after that day. This prevents remediation from dispatching the previously missed messages while obeying explicit no-send instructions, without disabling future event reminders or changing the queue. Do not manually invoke any sender or run a backfill.

## 2026-11-11 corrected root-cause and acceptance record

Full read-only Outbox search included the active `scheduled_fanout` source type, not only legacy `event_reminder`. Twenty Nov-10 fanout jobs were accepted (19 direct-chat plus one group chat, including admin/other recipients). Comparison by exact canonical supplier contact and event-specific message content found accepted event reminders for every other eligible supplier, including the photographer using a group. Specifically neither reported supplier had a matching Outbox job; **both phone strings ended with U+2069**. This is the direct, reproducible failure. Previous claim that all 14 lacked a reminder was wrong and is superseded.
- Production fanout source previously did `if (whatsappTarget.chatId) queue.push(...)`, thereby silently treating an invalid contact as a completed event fanout. New code additionally treats an explicitly enabled yet invalid destination as an enqueue failure, logs the event/supplier IDs without their phone, and does not finalize the pending source. Correcting that contact permits an idempotent future retry; already enqueued recipients are deduplicated, not sent twice. The phone parser strips the nonsemantic bidi marks in the two affected saved contacts without any data edits.
- The broader first-page pagination flaw was a *latent issue in alternate reminder paths*, not the explanation for the other suppliers on Nov 10. Their messages were indeed successfully enqueued and accepted from the scheduled-fanout path. Event-day hold continues through local Nov 11 and expires on Nov 12, obeying owner no-send for this already manually handled incident.
